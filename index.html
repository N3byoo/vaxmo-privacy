Vaxmo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

In short: Vaxmo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising.

What we collect

Nothing. Vaxmo has no user accounts and no backend service. We cannot see your tasks, and we do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

What the app stores, and where

Vaxmo saves the following in your device's private app storage. It never leaves your device:

  • Your tasks — titles, notes, priorities, due dates, and completion status
  • Your settings — reminder times and display preferences
  • Your answers to the short setup questions shown when you first open the app
  • Scheduled reminder identifiers, so reminders can be updated or cancelled

This data is sandboxed by Android: other apps cannot read it. Uninstalling Vaxmo permanently deletes all of it.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking components of any kind, and no third-party service receives your data because no data is ever transmitted.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by Android on your device. Vaxmo does not use push notifications, so no notification content is sent through any external service.

Permissions, and why they are needed

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show your reminders.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — to deliver reminders at the time you set. If you decline this, reminders still work but Android may deliver them a few minutes late.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore your scheduled reminders after your device restarts, so they are not silently lost.

The home screen widget

The optional widget reads your tasks from the same private on-device storage in order to display them. It transmits nothing.

Children

Vaxmo is a general-purpose task manager and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Deleting your data

Use Clear All inside the app, or uninstall Vaxmo. Either removes everything permanently. Because nothing is stored off your device, there is no server-side copy to request or erase.
